# Exciton Basis Truncation Verification

**Date:** 09-24-2026

### Context & Purpose
During finite-Q calculations for naphthalene, the Bethe-Salpeter Equation (BSE) absorption calculations were converged using a 16v x 16c basis (32 total bands)[cite: 18]. However, the electron-phonon coupling payload (`eph.h5`) provided by Sameer was extracted with only 12 total bands (6v + 6c). 

This utility verifies whether truncating the exciton eigenvectors down to that inner 6v x 6c subspace is physically justified, avoiding an unnecessary re-extraction or recalculation of the EPW electron-phonon matrix elements.

### Physics Check
Exciton wavefunctions are linear superpositions of electron-hole transitions normalized to unity:

$$\Sigma_{v, c, k} |A_{v,c,k}^S|^2 = 1.0$$

While many high-energy bands are required during the BSE diagonalization step to relax screening and converge exciton eigenvalues, bound states near the band edge typically concentrate most of their probability amplitude in the frontier bands. This script evaluates the norm inside the truncated subspace:

Retained % = (Truncated Norm / Total Norm) * 100

A retained norm >= 95% indicates the exciton can be safely truncated in `compute_xctph.py` without compromising the physics of the scattering elements.

### `xct.h5` Dataset Structure
The `xct.h5` file generated by `write_xct_h5.py` stores exciton states in `/exciton_data/eigenvectors` with the shape `(ns, nv, nc, nk, nevecs, nQ)`:
* `ns`: Spin components (1 for unpolarized).
* `nv`: Valence bands (16).
* `nc`: Conduction bands (16).
* `nk`: Electron momentum grid points (256).
* `nevecs`: Exciton states solved and stored (500).
* `nQ`: Exciton center-of-mass momentum points (6 along the b-path).

### Subspace Slicing
In BerkeleyGW, both valence and conduction bands in the `eigenvectors` tensor are indexed radiating outward from the fundamental gap:
* **Valence bands** are ordered downward from the Fermi level (index `0` is the VBM/HOMO, index `1` is HOMO-1, ..., index `15` is the deepest core band).
* **Conduction bands** are ordered upward from the Fermi level (index `0` is the CBM/LUMO, index `1` is LUMO+1, ..., index `15` is the highest conduction band).

To select the inner 12-band window closest to the band gap ($6v \times 6c$), slice both dimensions from the beginning:
* **Valence (top 6 bands, HOMO to HOMO-5):** `[:6]` (indices 0 through 5)
* **Conduction (bottom 6 bands, LUMO to LUMO+5):** `[:6]` (indices 0 through 5)

Results and retained percentages are exported directly to `norm-trunc-verif.out`.
